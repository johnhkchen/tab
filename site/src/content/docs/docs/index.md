Tab keeps the running tab for a group of people who share costs: who paid for
what, who owes whom, and what's been paid back. It doesn't move money; it keeps
the record straight.

## What works today

Version 0.4.0 runs a shared house and reads its bills. You can make a group,
invite people, add expenses split any way you like, see who owes whom, and
settle up. Bills that come back log in a tap, splits change while someone's
away, and you can ask for money with a message you copy. Forward a bill email
or add a photo or PDF, and Tab fills in the expense for you to check.

Using Tab from Claude comes next.
