Tab keeps the running tab for a group of people who share costs: who paid for
what, who owes whom, and what's been paid back. It doesn't move money; it keeps
the record straight.

## What works today

Version 0.1.0 is the foundation. You can run Tab, and it starts up cleanly with
its database ready, but you can't log expenses yet. Expenses, groups, and
balances come next.
