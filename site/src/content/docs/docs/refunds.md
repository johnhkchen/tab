---
title: Refunds
description: The full price back within 30 days of any payment, no questions.
---

If you paid for Plus in the last 30 days, you can have all of it back. You
don't need a reason.

## How to ask

- Open your plan page in Tab, choose **Manage billing**, and ask Paddle for a
  refund there, or
- Reply to your Paddle receipt, or email [[CONTACT EMAIL]].

Paddle returns the money to the card or account you paid with. Your account
moves to Hosted free, and you keep everything except bill reading.

## After 30 days

You can still cancel any time. Plus lasts until the end of the year you paid
for, and it won't renew.

## Deleting your account

Deleting your account cancels Plus at once, and if you paid in the last 30
days, Tab refunds that payment for you.
