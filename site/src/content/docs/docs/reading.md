---
title: Send Tab your bills
description: Forward a bill email or add a photo or PDF, and Tab fills in the expense for you to check.
---

Version 0.4.0 reads your bills. Send one in, and Tab fills in the amount, the
dates, and who it's from. Nothing counts until you check it and save it.

## Add a bill

Open **Bills to check** and pick **Take a photo or pick a file**. A photo of a
receipt or a PDF statement both work. Tab shrinks photos on your phone before
they upload.

## Forward bills by email

Each group gives you your own address, like
`elm-street-house-k3x9m2ab4q@bills.tab.b28.dev`. You'll find it under **Bills to
check**, then **Forward bills by email**. Your housemates each get their own.

Set up a filter in your email, and every new bill from that company lands in
Bills to check with the right group picked. The page has steps for Gmail,
Outlook.com, iCloud Mail, and Fastmail. Tab never asks for access to your
mailbox: it sees what you forward and nothing else.

Turning on forwarding in Gmail sends a code to your Tab address. Tab shows it on
the same page, so you can type it into Gmail.

## One bill, several emails

A utility might send three emails about one bill: the bill is ready, autopay is
scheduled, the payment went through. Tab puts them together into one bill to
check, along with the PDF if you add it. It matches them by company and amount,
or by the amount and the due date.

If an email shows up after you saved the bill, Tab adds it to that expense
instead of asking you again.

Tab can get it wrong. On a bill to check, **Same bill as another?** puts
two together, and **Not part of this bill** takes a document back out.

## Names a company goes by

The water company might sign its emails "SFPUC" and print "San Francisco Water
Power Sewer" on the bill. Each bill on the **Bills** page keeps a list of other
names it goes by, under **Also called**. Tab adds the names it sees whenever
you save a bill, and you can edit the list.

If a landlord or property manager forwards you bills for several companies, add
their address under **Who forwards bills to you** on the Bills page. Tab then
reads the company from the bill, not from them. Tab offers to add them when it
notices.

## What Tab keeps

Once you save or drop a bill, Tab deletes the photo, PDF, or email. The expense
keeps a note of each one: what it was, who sent it, the date, and the amount,
with a search line you can paste into Gmail to find the email again.

## If it can't read a bill

You get the same form with the original beside it, to fill in yourself. That
also happens when whoever runs Tab hasn't turned bill reading on.

A bill that says the account is past due gets a **Past due** flag, so you can
check that autopay is still on. A bill that carries a balance from last month
fills in this month's charges only, with the carried amount one tap away.

## Emails about it

Tab doesn't email you each bill. On the 10th and the 25th, one email lists what's
waiting. A bill due before the next one gets its own email. Pick other days on
your **You** page.

A bill that usually comes every month but hasn't shown up a week late appears in
that email too, and shows **Late** on the Bills page, in case autopay stopped.
