These terms cover Hosted Tab, the copy of Tab run at `app.tab.b28.dev`. If you
run Tab yourself, the open-source license (AGPLv3) covers you instead, and
none of this applies.

Hosted Tab is run by John Chen. To reach him, email
john@b28.dev.

## Your account

- One account per person, with an email address you can read. Sign-in links
  go there, so keep that inbox to yourself.
- You need to be 18 or older, or the age of majority where you live.
- Tell us if you think someone else got into your account.

## Plans

- **Hosted free** costs nothing and needs no card. You get everything in Tab
  except bill reading.
- **Plus** costs $10 a year. Tab reads up to 100 bills a month for you.
- **A new account** gets 3 months of Plus. You can ask for 6 more months at a
  time by answering one question. That never needs a card, and it never turns
  into a paid plan by itself.
- **Reading limits:** when your month's bills are read, new ones wait until
  the 1st. Tab never charges more for reading.

The [pricing page](/pricing) lists every plan and what it includes.

## Paying

Paddle sells Plus for us as the merchant of record, so your payment, taxes,
and receipts go through Paddle, and
[Paddle's buyer terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/invoiced-consumer-terms)
apply to the payment.

- Plus renews each year until you cancel. Paddle emails you before it renews.
- Cancel any time from **Manage billing** on your plan page. Plus lasts to the
  end of the year you paid for, then your account moves to Hosted free.
- Changes to the price apply from your next renewal, and we tell you at least
  30 days before.
- [Refunds](/docs/refunds/): the full price, within 30 days of any payment.

## Your data

Your expenses, bills, and groups are yours. Tab doesn't sell them, doesn't
show ads, and doesn't use them to train anyone's AI. The
[privacy page](/docs/privacy/) says who can see what.

- **Download everything** on your You page gives you a CSV any time.
- **Delete my account** on your You page removes you and your private data.
  Shared expenses stay with each group under "Former member", so your
  housemates' balances stay right.

## Using it fairly

Don't use Tab to break the law, to harass anyone, or to send other people
email they didn't ask for. Don't try to get into data that isn't yours, or to
overload the service. We can close an account that does these things, and
refund any unused months.

## If something goes wrong

We look after Hosted Tab carefully: backups every night, and a copy of each
change kept off the server. Still, it's offered as it is, without a promise
that it never goes down or never has a bug. Tab keeps a record of who owes
whom; it doesn't move money, and it isn't financial advice.

As far as the law allows, our responsibility for any problem is limited to
what you paid for Tab in the 12 months before it.

## If Hosted Tab closes

We'd tell you at least 60 days ahead, keep **Download everything** working
until the end, and refund the months you paid for and didn't get. Tab is open
source, so you could also keep running it yourself.

## Changes to these terms

We'll post changes here and email you about anything that matters at least
30 days before it applies. These terms follow the law of
California, United States.
