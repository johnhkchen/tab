---
title: Bills, time away, and asking for it
description: Log rent in a tap, change the split while someone's away, and ask for money with a message you copy.
---

Version 0.3.0 adds the tools a shared house reaches for every month. You'll
find each one on your group's page.

## Bills

Rent, internet, electric: set a bill up once on the **Bills** page. Give it a
name, how often it comes, who usually pays, and how to split it. If autopay
takes it on the same day each month, add that day too.

When a new one arrives, press **Log it**. The add-expense form opens with the
last amount, the autopay date, and the dates the bill covers. Change what's
different and save. Nothing counts until you save it.

A bill that stays the same, like rent, asks you to tick a box if the amount
changes. That stops a typo from turning $1,800 into $18,000.

A bill without its own split goes evenly among everyone in the group.

## When someone's away

Open **Temporary splits** from the group page. Under **Someone's away?** pick
the person, then the dates and the bills it covers. Tab proposes a split
without them, and you can change it before you save. Only admins can set one
up.

It stops on its own when the dates end.

If a bill covers part of the time away, the add-expense form works out each
share by day and says why. Say the electric bill covers 30 days and Jo was
away for 10 of them: Jo pays for 20 days, not 30.

Two temporary splits on the same bills and the same days can't both apply.
Tab shows you the overlap and a combined version, and saves it once you
confirm.

## How long money waits

The group page shows, for each person, the average number of days between
paying for everyone and getting paid back. Each expense shows it per share,
like "paid back after 9 days". Payments count against the oldest shares first.

It counts days only. Tab never adds interest.

## Ask for it

Next to anyone who owes you, **Ask for it** opens three messages: a Venmo note,
a text, and an email. Each one lists what they owe and how to pay you. Copy one
and send it from your own phone. Tab doesn't send anything for you.

## Statement links

On the same page you can add a statement link: one person's month, on a page
they can open without signing in. It shows their own bills and payments, and
nobody else's. It never shows private expenses.

A link works for 30 days. Anyone in the group can turn it off sooner, and it
stays off. Tab shows you the link once, so copy it then.
